# Attribution

This repository implements the SAPP manuscript and the comparators listed in [docs/baselines.md](docs/baselines.md). Their papers are cited in `paper/references.bib`.

NIST channel records and simulator scenario files derive from the NIST quasi-deterministic channel generator. Fraunhofer UWB arrays derive from the Fraunhofer IIS fingerprinting dataset for positioning. Their provenance is recorded in [docs/data-and-api.md](docs/data-and-api.md#data-provenance). Dataset access and redistribution are governed by the original providers' terms; this repository does not assign a new license to those materials.

NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Numba, PyTorch, uv, pytest, Ruff, and TeX Live are external dependencies. Their licenses accompany their respective distributions.
